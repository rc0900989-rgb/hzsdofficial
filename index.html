Questions, answered

Everything you were
about to ask.

Still unsure? Write to hello@hertzsync.audio — a human who cuts sound replies.

Does Hertz Sync really work with the network off?

Yes. The neural models, UCS taxonomy, and search index all live on your drive. License activation is an offline key exchange — no account, no cloud sync, no background pings. You can run it on a permanently air-gapped machine.

Which formats and sample rates are supported?

WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG, and RF64 read natively, up to 384 kHz / 32-bit float. Export targets include WAV, AIFF, FLAC, and MP3 with dithering options.

How does the AI decide on a UCS category?

It combines filename and folder semantics with embedded metadata and an audio embedding pass, then scores candidates against the UCS 8.2 tree. Every suggestion lands in a review queue — nothing is written to your files without approval.

Will it modify or move my original files?

Never by default. Scanning is read-only, and tags can be stored in the local database or written to sidecar files. Any destructive operation — rename, reshape, move — is explicit, previewable, and reversible.

Does it integrate with my DAW or asset pipeline?

Drag and drop into Pro Tools, Nuendo, Reaper, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Resolve, Unity, Unreal, Wwise, and FMOD. Metadata exports as BWF/iXML, CSV manifests, or Soundminer-compatible sidecars.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 12 Monterey or Windows 10 (21H2)+, 8 GB RAM, 2 GB free disk for the app and models. Apple Silicon, Intel, and AMD are all native. A discrete GPU speeds up long indexing passes but is not required.
