FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it change or move my sound files?

Not unless you ask it to. Categories, favorites and metadata edits live inside Hertz Sync Sound Design. When you write metadata or export files, you choose exactly what gets written and where — the originals stay put.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Scanning, the local index, search and audition all run on your computer, and the app works offline. Online, it only checks your license key and asks GitHub whether a newer version is out.

How is this different from Hertz Sync?

Hertz Sync Sound Design is the sibling app built for sound designers: UCS category review, metadata cleanup, batch processing and Reaper delivery for game and film work. Hertz Sync focuses on music production libraries. Pro keys are per-product.

Who makes Hertz Sync Sound Design?

I'm Quillon Holden, an independent developer under the label Vykra. I built Hertz Sync Sound Design because my own SFX library had become impossible to search, and finding the right riser took longer than spotting it. Questions or ideas? Reply to your purchase email and it comes straight to me.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Within 7 days of buying Pro, reply to your purchase email and you'll get a full refund, no reason needed. Your key stops working right after the refund.

What’s new in 1.0.5?

Delivery is now one screen: choose a selection, a folder or the whole library, see every new name and every skip before anything is written, then name, tag, copy (or rename in place), write the manifest and undo the whole run as one batch. Progress and Stop are shown while it runs, the free plan’s daily limit is shown before the run, and a finished delivery can still be undone after a restart. Delivery of 2,000 small sounds now takes about 34 seconds instead of minutes. Full notes are on the release page.
